Examining the 4 critical vectors of evidence exposure—from physical transit to siloed SQL databases—and how decentralized cryptographic anchoring guarantees non-repudiation across law enforcement, forensic science, and the judiciary.

Current Fragmented Legacy Framework

Vulnerable to Undetected Tampering
1. Physical Paperwork & Wax Seals

FIRs and witness statements exist on physical pulp sheets. Wax seals on evidence bags can be softened with thermal tools, contents substituted, and resealed without digital verification.

2. Disconnected Departmental Databases

Police CCTNS, Forensic LIMS, and Court e-Filing CIS run in separate network silos. Database administrators or rogue insiders can execute direct SQL updates (`UPDATE fir SET ...`) leaving zero inter-agency trace.

3. Courier Transit Latency & Handover Blindspots

Physical evidence envelopes travel via couriers over 5-15 days. Handover registers rely on handwritten logbooks where signatures can be forged, and lost items are only noticed weeks later in court.

4. Section 65B Electronic Admissibility Disputes

Defense counsels routinely challenge digital copies (CCTV footage, CDR logs, WhatsApp exports) due to unproven certificate chains and lack of mathematical immutability proofs.

Aegis-Veritas Cryptographic Ledger

3. Continuous Real-Time Handover Telemetry

When evidence moves from Police to FSL, receiving scientists scan the physical QR tag; Aegis-Veritas verifies the manifest hash against the seizure entry within seconds. Discrepancies trigger automated court alerts.
